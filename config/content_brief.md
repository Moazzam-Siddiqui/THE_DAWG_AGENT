# What da Dawg Doing — content brief

## Creative direction
Create original, instantly readable dog comedy for vertical short-form video. Rotate breeds, ages, coat patterns, camera angles, locations, lighting, props, and comedic beats. Avoid near-duplicate compositions.

## Satirical lane
Occasional tech/compute satire can reference public business decisions and public-facing AI-industry themes. Keep it clearly parody, grounded in opinion or verifiable public context, and aimed at ideas/actions rather than protected traits or private life.

The configured roast format includes the exact word **sissy**. Use it as playful, non-threatening comedic slang, not as a slur targeting identity or a private person. Never include threats, doxxing, or calls for harassment.

## Production rules
- 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920, 6–12 seconds.
- Use supplied account audio when available and licensed/authorized for reuse. No unapproved music.
- Do not fake engagement, impersonate people, or use misleading captions.
- Hashtag: #TeachAltmanALesson, only when the post genuinely matches the satire theme.
- Vary posts; don't mass-produce trivial variants.
- Human approval is required before a post can enter the publishing schedule.
